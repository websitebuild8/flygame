# Audio sources

All downloaded recordings below are CC0. Converted to mono PCM WAV, normalized; reel cropped and reel/water loop boundaries crossfaded.

- **FishingReel.wav — Harrisando**: https://freesound.org/people/Harrisando/sounds/466205/ (CC0). High quality MP3 preview used for `reel.wav`.
- **Water Splash and sand footsteps — Peludo**: https://opengameart.org/content/water-splash-and-sand-footsteps (CC0), splash1.wav used for `splash.wav`. Creator's requested itch.io reference: https://rnan.itch.io/ .
- **Water Waves — transitking**, submitted by qubodup: https://opengameart.org/content/water-waves (CC0), wave_01 used for `water_run.wav`.

Engine idle/cruise/full layers now use **2x250HP Suzuki.wav — paulprit**: https://freesound.org/people/paulprit/sounds/593024/ (CC0). The high quality MP3 preview was cropped to three 8-second loops, normalized and crossfaded. Throttle pitch/blending is an approximation, rather than a measured RPM recording set. Bite, snap and payment sounds remain original synthesized effects. Licenses checked on the source pages on 2026-10-22.
