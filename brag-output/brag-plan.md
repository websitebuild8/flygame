# Brag Plan: Ishaq's Fishing Trip

## Invocation
Professional/polished, landscape 1920×1080, exactly 20 seconds. Music and sparse
SFX enabled. Narration disabled. Local render only; no publication.

## Nine-question planning rubric
1. **App:** An offline Godot mobile fishing adventure set around Kelaa and four neighbouring Haa Alif islands, with hands-on boat driving, first-person fishing and island markets.
2. **Impressive claim:** The game's own line: “A small boat. An ocean of possibilities.”
3. **Visual hook:** The actual Kelaa island and enclosed harbour, surrounded by navy offshore water and teal shallows, seen in a moving aerial shot.
4. **Actual UI:** Rod/reel casting and catch landing, followed by the first-person market sale. Use actual engine captures, not redesigned pretend screens.
5. **Shortest satisfying edit:** 20 seconds, explicitly requested.
6. **Tone:** polished; a warm, premium island-adventure launch film. Calm, confident text, clean motion, restrained transitions.
7. **Audio:** Warm, clean music bed with two subtle motion accents and a tiny splash cue. No narration, no hype impacts.
8. **Share caption:** Ishaq's Fishing Trip: set sail from Kelaa, reel in your catch and trade at island markets. Work-in-progress mobile gameplay.
9. **User flow:** Set sail → cast/reel/land → sell the catch and prepare the next trip.

## Angle and hook
The Maldives await. Lead with a real moving island shot and the game's own
opening copy. Move from the scale of the islands into the tactile actions of
driving and fishing, ending on the project's name and original tagline.

and Open Sans for supporting labels, selected for a similar clean, rounded feel.

Small supporting label “Your boat. Your course.” No misleading autopilot claim.
Sequential/interaction: boat movement is real gameplay. One soft accent at arrival.
Transition: footage becomes a framed gameplay panel on the navy stage.
### 3. Cast. Reel. Land. — 6.50–11.50 (5.00 s)

catch duration. One understated splash cue. Beat emphasis near 8.74 s.
Transition: directional wipe into the market's matching first-person view.
